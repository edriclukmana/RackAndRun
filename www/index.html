Rack & Run

Build runs and groups and be the first to empty your rack. Played by the classic Rummikub rules, with 2 to 4 players in any mix of people and bots.

Play against bots, or pass one phone, tablet or computer between players. Each person’s tiles stay hidden until they tap to see them.

Waiting for players

Everyone opens Rack & Run on their own phone, tablet or computer, taps Players, chooses Separate devices, and joins this table.

    How to play

    Be the first player to lay down every tile on your rack.

    The tiles

    There are 106 tiles: the numbers 1 to 13 in red, blue, orange and black, two of each, plus two jokers. Everyone starts with 14 tiles. The rest form the face-down pool.

    Sets

    • A run is three or more consecutive numbers in one color, like blue 4, 5, 6. A 1 can’t follow a 13.
    • A group is three or four tiles of the same number, each in a different color.

    Your opening

    Your first play must be one or more new sets made only from your own tiles, worth at least 30 points in total (each tile counts its number). Until you’ve opened, you can’t touch tiles on the table.

    Each turn

    Play at least one tile from your rack, then press Done. If you can’t play, or would rather not, draw a tile instead. Drawing ends your turn, so you can’t play the tile you just drew. When the pool is empty, you pass.

    Rearranging the table

    After your opening, you can split, combine and rebuild any sets on the table, as long as every set is valid when you press Done and at least one tile came from your rack. Tiles that were already on the table can’t go back to your rack.

    Jokers

    A joker stands in for any tile. In your opening it counts as the tile it replaces. You can take a joker from the table by putting the tile it stands for in its place, then use the joker in another set the same turn.

    Scoring

    When someone empties their rack, everyone else loses the value of the tiles still on their rack (a joker costs 30) and the winner gains the total. If the pool runs out and nobody can play, the lowest rack wins. A round is one game per player; the most games won takes the match, with points as the tiebreaker.

    Playing together

    • One device: set two or more seats to Player. When it’s someone’s turn, hand them the device; they tap to reveal their tiles.
    • Separate devices: each person opens Rack & Run on their own device, as the Android app or the website. One person creates a table, the others join it from the list. Moves show up on every screen as they happen.
    • Each device is its own player, so use the same phone or computer to come back to a game you left.
    • Need to go? Players, then Leave table, hands your seat to a bot so the others can keep playing.

    Using the table

    • Tap or click tiles to select them, then press + on a set to add them, or New set to start one. You can also drag tiles, including several selected tiles at once.
    • A teal bar marks tiles you played this turn. Those can still go back to your rack.
    • Gold rings show what others played since your last turn, and the tile you last drew.
    • 777 sorts your rack into groups and 789 into runs. You can drag tiles within your rack to arrange them.
    • Undo puts everything back as it was at the start of your turn.
    • Hint makes playable tiles glow. Press it again to lay the play out for you.
    • With the turn timer on, running out of time draws a tile for you. If your move was unfinished, the table is restored and you draw three tiles.
